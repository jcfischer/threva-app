# Week

Click Week between Day and Everything. The seven columns start today; scroll the grid horizontally on a phone.

`bun run verify:ui week` creates a dated row through the isolated stack's real outline API, checks the sample calendar events on days six and seven, records wide and narrow views, and clicks the row to open its Everything page. It then intercepts only the outline read and the Desk read separately to prove partial failures retain the other source. These failures are synthetic; the calendar and outline setup use the real isolated development stack. Routines stay out of this view.

Phone screenshots capture the viewport: Chromium full-page capture can lose painted content outside a horizontally scrolled grid even while its bounds are unchanged. The journey also asserts window scroll remains zero, the body stays within viewport width, and the header and view switch remain visible after panning to the last day.
